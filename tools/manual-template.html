1What Talanoa is

Talanoa helps a person who can't speak tell you what they want, how they feel, and who they want to see. It was made for the residents of Faleofaz.

It works like a set of talking picture cards on a phone (or a tablet). Every picture says a short sentence out loud in a natural voice, for example the carrot picture says "I want a snack." The words also appear in big letters at the top, so you can read them from across the room, and the picture that said them glows for 30 seconds.

There are {{PAGE_COUNT}} pages with {{TILE_COUNT}} pictures in total, plus six important buttons that are always on screen.

Three promises the app keeps
  • It never stays silent. If a voice recording can't play, the phone's own voice says it instead.
  • The top row never moves. Its six buttons are always in the same place, so they can be found without looking.
  • It works without internet once it has been set up.

2Setting up a phone

You only do this once per phone (a tablet works the same way). It takes about two minutes and needs Wi-Fi.

  1. 1
    Open Google Chrome

    Use Chrome, not a preview from email or Google Drive.

  2. 2
    Go to the Talanoa address

    Scan the QR code on the front of this guide with the phone's camera, or type tcdeveloper27.github.io/Talanoa (capital T in Talanoa).

  3. 3
    Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top right)

    Choose Add to Home screen or Install app, then confirm.

  4. 4
    Open Talanoa from its new icon

    It opens full screen like any other app.

  5. 5
    Leave it on Wi-Fi for a minute

    It saves the pictures and voice onto the phone. Check in Settings: Status should say ✓ Ready to use with no internet.

4Talking with it

Tap a picture once and the phone says the words.

  • 👆
    One tap = one sentence

    No double-tapping needed. A tap still counts if the finger slides a little or rests on the picture for a while.

  • 🔁
    Tap again to repeat

    It starts again from the beginning.

  • ⚡
    Quick taps are fine

    A new tap stops the last sentence and says the new one. Nothing gets stuck.

  • 📳
    A little buzz

    The phone vibrates slightly on each tap, if it can.

  • ✨
    Show someone

    The picture glows for 30 seconds, long enough to lift the phone and show a person what was said.

On the Maverik page, after tapping Drink. The top says "I would like a drink", and Drink glows.

5Moving between pages

Use the big ◀ ▶ arrows to flip through the pages, like turning the pages of a book, or swipe sideways across the pictures.

  • 👈
    Swipe to turn the page

    Slide a finger sideways across the pictures, like on a phone's home screen. The page follows the finger; let go early and it springs back. A swipe never says anything.

  • ◀▶
    Or tap the arrows

    Each arrow shows a small picture of the page it goes to. After the last page it goes back to the first.

  • 🗂️
    Jump straight to a page

    Tap the page name in the middle. All pages appear as big buttons: tap one to go there, or tap ✕ Close.

Pages turning by accident?Swiping can be turned off in Settings. The arrows always work.

6The Mom & Dad page

All the parent and grandparent tiles are together on one page.

Every Dad tile has the matching Mom tile directly underneath it: Dad and Mom, Call Dad and Call Mom, Dad's house and Mom's house, Dad coming? and Mom coming? The bottom row has Grandma, Grandpa, Nana and Papa.

This layout is deliberateThe family asked that Mom always appears under Dad. The app is built so this can't be changed by accident.
When a resident taps "Call Dad" or "Call Mom"Treat it as a real request: tell them what will happen ("Let's call Mom after lunch") so they know they were heard.

9Without Wi-Fi

Once the phone shows ✓ Ready to use with no internet, Talanoa works anywhere: on outings, in the car, at the store, or when the Wi-Fi is down. Pictures, words and the chosen voice are all saved on the phone.

After choosing a new voiceKeep the phone on Wi-Fi for a minute so the new voice can be saved. Until then, any sentence that isn't saved yet uses the phone's own voice. It's never silent.

10Tips for helping residents

A talking board works best when the people around it use it too.

  1. 🗣️
    Show, don't test

    While you talk, tap the pictures yourself ("Let's have lunch", tapping Lunch). This is how people learn where things are. Never quiz them.

  2. ⏳
    Give time

    Wait after asking a question. Finding the right picture can take a while.

  3. 💬
    Every tap counts

    Answer what was said, even if you think it was an accident. That's how they learn their words have power.

  4. 📍
    Keep it close and charged

    The board only helps if it's within reach, all day, in every room.

  5. 🤝
    Tell the family

    Let the family know which pictures get used, and which words are missing.

Talanoa supports, it doesn't replaceTalanoa is a simple, friendly board, not a full professional speech system. A speech-language pathologist can suggest what each resident needs.

11If something goes wrong

What you noticeWhat to do
No sound at allTurn the volume up with the phone's side buttons. Check the phone isn't on silent. In Settings, check Volume isn't low.
The voice sounds roboticIn Settings, Built-in voice is probably chosen. Choose Michael (or another named voice).
Only a brown bar, no picturesIt was opened in a preview (from email or Google Drive). Open Talanoa from its home-screen icon, or in Chrome.
A page is missingSettings → Pages to show → tick it.
Settings won't openPress and hold the ⚙ for a full second. A quick tap does nothing, on purpose.
New words from the family aren't showingConnect to Wi-Fi, then Settings → Check for updates now.
"404 / There isn't a GitHub Pages site here"The address was typed wrong. Use the QR code, or type tcdeveloper27.github.io/Talanoa with a capital T.
The ▶ arrow (or a swipe) jumped back to "I want"That's normal: after the last page it starts again at the first.
Pages keep turning by accidentSettings → untick Swipe to change pages. The ◀ ▶ arrows still work.
A picture is glowingThat's the last picture tapped. It stops glowing by itself after 30 seconds, or as soon as another picture is tapped.
The screen keeps turning offSee "Screen timeout" in section 12.
Something else looks wrongClose Talanoa completely and open it again from its icon. If it still happens, take a photo of the screen and email it to Tim Broussard at tcdeveloper@gmail.com.

12Looking after the phone

  • 🔋
    Charge every night

    A flat battery means a resident with no voice.

  • ⏲️
    Screen timeout

    On most Android phones: Settings → Display → Screen timeout, and choose a longer time (for example 10 minutes).

  • 🔊
    Volume

    Use the side buttons while Talanoa is talking to set how loud it is.

  • 🧽
    Clean and protect

    Wipe the screen with a slightly damp cloth. A sturdy case is a good idea.

13Asking for changes

New words, different wording, a real photo of someone instead of a picture: the family can add these. Write down what's needed (for example "add Swing, 'I want to go on the swing'") and pass it to Tim Broussard. The change reaches the phone automatically, usually the same day.
